# Private Intelligence

Private Intelligence is a methodology for autonomous agents to execute complex engineering and research tasks with the discipline of a principal engineer. It moves beyond simple prompt-response cycles into structured execution, verification, and hardening.

## Core Execution Workflow

Follow this lifecycle for every major task:

1. **Intelligence Gathering**: Use `research-and-summarize` and `think-before-coding` to define the problem space and constraints.
2. **Strategic Planning**: Use `task-decomposition` and `idea-to-spec` to create a verifiable execution plan.
3. **Robust Implementation**: Apply `simplicity-first`, `api-design`, and `incremental-coding`.
4. **Verification & Hardening**: Execute `test-driven-development`, `security-hardening`, and `hallucination-prevention`.
5. **Production Shipping**: Follow `production-deployment` and `git-workflow` for zero-downtime releases.
